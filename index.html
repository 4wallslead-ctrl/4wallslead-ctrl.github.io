From requirement to keys in hand.

Five clear stages, tracked live in the app. You always know what happens next.

  1. 01 Register A landlord lists a space, or a business posts what it needs.
  2. 02 Review Our team checks every listing and requirement before it goes live.
  3. 03 Match Spaces and businesses are paired on type, location, size and budget.
  4. 04 Visit & offer Book site visits, express interest and negotiate, all in one place.
  5. 05 Close Our team guides the agreement to signature and handover.
