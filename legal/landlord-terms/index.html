Landlord Listing Terms

Last updated: 2 November 2026

These terms apply when you list a property on 4WALLS. They add to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, which also apply.

1. Your right to list

You confirm that you own the property, or have the owner's written authority to list it and negotiate for them, and that letting or selling it on the terms you list is lawful.

2. Accurate listings

  • Describe the property truthfully: location, size, price, deposit, maintenance charges, availability, condition and permitted use.
  • Upload only your own photos, or photos you have permission to use, that show the actual property.
  • Keep the listing up to date, and remove it in the app once the property is no longer available.

3. Approvals and compliance

You are responsible for the property's approvals and for anything the law requires of a landlord or seller, including building and occupancy approvals, permitted use, property tax, and stamping and registration of any agreement you sign.

4. Review by 4WALLS

The 4WALLS team reviews every listing before it goes live and may ask for changes. We may decline or remove a listing that is inaccurate, unlawful, duplicated or unsafe. Approval does not mean we have verified the property.

5. Visits and offers

Respond to visit requests in the app, give access at confirmed times, and keep the site safe. Offers you make or accept in the app are not binding until a written agreement is signed.

6. Fees

Listing is free. If 4WALLS acts as broker for a deal on your property, the commission and who pays it are agreed in writing for that deal, and its terms (including not bypassing 4WALLS with a tenant it introduced) apply.

7. Removing a listing or leaving

You can remove a listing at any time from the property's page. If you delete your account, listings that were never published are deleted; listings that are part of a visit, offer or deal are kept with your identity removed and taken off the market, as the Privacy Policy explains.
