Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 4WALLS PREMIUM REALTY ("4WALLS", "we"), No. 262, Kumaran Nagar, Sholavaram 7th Cross Street, Chennai, Tamil Nadu - 600067, collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the 4WALLS app and website. We process personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and their rules. For this data, 4WALLS is the Data Fiduciary.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: name, mobile number, email address, date of birth (to confirm you are an adult), your role (landlord or tenant), and your password, which is stored only as a secure hash. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Device notification token: if you allow notifications, a token that identifies your phone so we can deliver alerts (for example a new match or a reply) to it. It is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.
  • Verification: the fact that your mobile number and email were verified, and records of the one-time codes sent to you.
  • Consent records: which version of these documents you accepted and when.
  • Listings: property type, address, locality, city, GPS location (only if you choose to tag it), size, price, deposit, terms, features, description and photos.
  • Requirements: the kind of space you need, preferred locations, size, budget and other details you enter.
  • Activity: matches, saved properties, site-visit requests and times, expressions of interest, offers and counter-offers, deal progress, and the notifications we send you.
  • Messages you send to and receive from the 4WALLS team in the app.
  • Technical data: device model, operating system, app version, and crash and error reports. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

We do not collect government ID numbers, bank or card details, or biometric data in the app.

2. Device permissions

  • Location: only when you choose to tag a property's position while listing it. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Camera and photos: only to add photos to a listing.

You can refuse or withdraw these permissions in your phone's settings; you can still enter an address by hand.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and verify your phone number.
  • To review listings and requirements, suggest matches, and help you arrange visits, express interest, make offers and close deals.
  • To send you service messages: one-time codes by SMS to verify your mobile number; verification and password-reset codes by email; one welcome message on WhatsApp after your number is verified; and notifications and replies from the 4WALLS team in the app.
  • We do not send promotional SMS, WhatsApp messages or calls. The 4WALLS team may call or message you only about a listing, requirement, visit, offer or deal you are pursuing. To stop WhatsApp messages, write to support@4wallsre.com; codes you ask for to sign in will still be sent.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and misuse, and to fix errors.
  • To keep records the law requires, and to handle complaints and disputes.

We rely on the consent you give when you create an account, and on the legitimate uses the law allows (such as complying with legal obligations). We do not sell your personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who sees your data

  • The other party in a match sees what they need to decide: the listing or requirement details, proposed visit times, interest and offer amounts. The app does not show your phone number or email to the other party. The 4WALLS team may share contact details with them only to arrange a visit or complete a deal you are pursuing.
  • The 4WALLS team sees your account, listings, requirements, activity and messages to run the Service.
  • Service providers who process data for us, only for these purposes:
  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage and live updates.
  • MSG91 (Walkover Web Solutions, India): sends SMS verification codes and WhatsApp messages. It receives your mobile number and the message text.
  • Our email delivery provider: verification and password-reset emails.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications to your phone. It receives your device notification token and the notification text.
  • Sentry: crash and error reports (servers in Germany).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile (Cloudflare, Inc.): bot protection at sign-in. It may receive your device's IP address and browser signals.
  • Google: only if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: map tiles and address search. It receives the address text you search for and your device's IP address.
  • Authorities, when the law or a valid legal request requires it, or to protect people's safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if the 4WALLS business is transferred, under the same protections.

5. Where your data is stored

Some of our service providers store or process data outside India. We transfer data only as permitted by Indian law and to providers bound to protect it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your account details: while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account: your name, phone number, email, photo, Google link and sign-in sessions are erased or replaced at once, and your saved properties and notifications are deleted.
  • Requirements that never led to a visit, interest, offer or deal, and listings that were never published, are deleted with your account. Their photos are deleted, or made permanently inaccessible and then removed from storage.
  • Visits, interests, offers, messages, deals and the listings or requirements they belong to involve another person. We keep them with your identity removed (shown as "Deleted account") for up to 8 years after the last activity, to resolve disputes and meet tax and accounting law, and then delete them. Any listing or requirement we keep is taken off the market.
  • A record that your deletion was completed, without your personal details, for up to 8 years.
  • Crash and error reports: up to 90 days.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or update it (most details can be edited in the app);
  • have it erased, by deleting your account in the app or at /legal/account-deletion/;
  • withdraw consent (we will then have to close your account, as the Service cannot run without the data above);
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become incapacitated;
  • have a grievance addressed by our Grievance Officer, and then complain to the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

Write to support@4wallsre.com. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit; passwords are hashed; sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage; listing photos sit in private storage served through short-lived links; and database rules limit each person to the data they are allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

The Service is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, tell us at support@4wallsre.com and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We will show the date of the latest version at the top and tell you in the app before a significant change takes effect.
