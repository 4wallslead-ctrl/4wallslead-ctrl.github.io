Grievance Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

If you have a complaint about 4WALLS, a listing, another user or how we handle your personal data, this is how to raise it and what we will do.

1. Grievance Officer

Grievance Officer, 4WALLS PREMIUM REALTY, support@4wallsre.com, 4WALLS PREMIUM REALTY, No. 262, Kumaran Nagar, Sholavaram 7th Cross Street, Chennai, Tamil Nadu - 600067. You can also write to support@4wallsre.com; data-protection complaints can go to support@4wallsre.com.

2. How to complain

Tell us your name and the mobile number or email on your account, what happened and when, the listing, visit or offer it concerns, and what you would like us to do. Screenshots help.

3. What happens next

  • We acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours.
  • We aim to resolve it within 30 days and tell you the outcome and the reasons.
  • Fraud, safety threats or a misused account are handled first; we may suspend the account or listing involved while we look into it.

4. Complaints about another user

We review the listing, requirement, visit or offer records in the app and may edit or remove content, or suspend an account. We cannot settle disputes about an agreement you signed with another user; those are between you and them.

5. If you are not satisfied

You may approach the consumer forum with jurisdiction, and for personal-data matters the Data Protection Board of India, after using this process.
