Delete Your 4WALLS Account

Last updated: 2 November 2026

You can delete your 4WALLS account and its personal data at any time. This page explains how and what happens to your data.

How to delete your account

  • In the app: Profile → Delete account. Confirm, and it is done immediately.
  • Can't open the app? Email support@4wallsre.com from the email address, or quoting the mobile number, on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm your identity and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email address, profile photo and Google sign-in link.
  • Your saved properties and notifications.
  • Requirements that never led to a visit, interest, offer or deal.
  • Listings that were never published. Their photos are deleted, or made permanently inaccessible and then removed from storage.
  • Your ability to sign in. This cannot be undone.

What is kept, and for how long

Site visits, interests, offers, messages, deals and the listings or requirements they belong to also concern the other party. They are kept with your identity removed (shown as "Deleted account") and taken off the market, for up to 8 years after the last activity, to resolve disputes and meet tax and accounting law. A record that your deletion was completed is kept without your personal details.
