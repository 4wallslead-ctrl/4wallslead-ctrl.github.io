Tenant Requirement Terms

Last updated: 2 November 2026

These terms apply when you post a requirement or pursue a property on 4WALLS. They add to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy, which also apply.

1. Accurate requirements

Describe what you genuinely need. If you act for a business, you confirm you are authorised to search, visit and make offers for it.

2. Your own checks

Before you sign or pay, check the property, the owner's title and authority, approvals and permitted use, and the agreement itself. 4WALLS does not verify these for you.

3. Visits and offers

Attend visits you confirm, or cancel in the app. Offers and counter-offers in the app record what was proposed; nothing is binding until both sides sign a written agreement.

4. Payments

4WALLS does not collect rent, deposits or advances through the app. Pay them only to the landlord under your signed agreement, never into a personal account of someone claiming to represent 4WALLS.

5. Fees

Posting a requirement and being matched are free. If 4WALLS acts as broker for your deal, the commission and who pays it are agreed in writing for that deal.

6. Pausing, closing or leaving

You can pause, mark fulfilled, archive or (before it is published) delete a requirement in the app. Visits, interests and offers already made are kept for both sides. If you delete your account, requirements that never led to a visit, interest or offer are deleted; the rest are kept with your identity removed, as the Privacy Policy explains.
